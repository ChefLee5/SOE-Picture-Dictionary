## 1. Audit Overview & Diagnosis Objective

The 14 image files inside `Meta ad mocks` were generated during initial brainstorming and prototyping. While they establish key conceptual directions, they contain common generative prototyping flaws (distorted facial details, muddy dark lighting, unbranded color schemes, off-model costumes, or generic stock appearances) that prevent them from being used directly as commercial ad creatives.

This audit deconstructs each mock's **conceptual intent** and defines the **exact upgrades** required for market-ready regeneration.

## 3. Universal Technical & Aesthetic Regeneration Rules

1. **Aesthetic Tone:** **Bright & Playful** — Soft cream `#FFF8F0` backgrounds, rich golden-orange `#FF6F00` accents, warm morning sunlight, natural wood, and high-fidelity 3D Pixar/DreamWorks character styling.
2. **Character Canon:** All 15 heroes and Seriphia must match the July 2026 Model Sheets 100%. No alterations to skin tones, hair textures, or signature costumes.
3. **Typography & Text Overlay:** Keep raw image backgrounds 100% clean and free of gibberish text. All headlines and CTAs will be applied as clean UI overlays or via Meta Ads Manager.
4. **Export Dimensions:**
   - **Feed Placements (1:1):** Minimum `1080 x 1080 px` (recommended `2048 x 2048 px`).
   - **Story & Reel Placements (9:16):** Minimum `1080 x 1920 px`.
