## 1. The 8-Point Canonical Quality Gate

Every visual asset submitted for Meta Ads, landing pages, or store delivery must pass all 8 checkpoints:

| # | Quality Check | Canonical Specification | Violation Example (Reject) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Animation Style** | **Luminous 2D/2.5D Storybook Digital Illustration** with clean line art, warm soft cel-shading, and organic painterly textures. | Shiny 3D CGI plastic, uncanny photorealistic faces, or cheap flat clipart. |
| **2** | **Seriphia Likeness** | Regal Black woman, warm golden-brown skin, voluminous dark-brown curly hair, **round gold-rimmed wire glasses**, **thin floating gold halo**, flowing **cream/ivory gown with gold scrollwork embroidery**. | Missing halo, missing glasses, non-canonical gown color, inaccurate hair texture. |
| **3** | **Harmonia Heroes** | **Kenji:** Spiky dark hair, **blue headband**, orange hoodie (white drawstrings), denim shorts, orange low-tops.<br>**Aiko:** Straight black hair in **high twin pigtails with orange bows**, blunt bangs, grassy-green A-line dress, **yellow/orange striped tights**, black mary-janes. | Altering Kenji's headband color or Aiko's pigtail ribbons/striped tights. |
| **4** | **Numeria Heroes** | **Kwame:** Short curl fade, **charcoal tech tracksuit with glowing cyan/neon-blue geometric lines**, fingerless tech gloves, cyan-soled boots.<br>**Octavia:** Rich dark skin, **side Dutch braid with pink bow**, light pink flared dress, pink flats. | Removing Kwame's cyan tech lines or Octavia's pink bow/side braid. |
| **5** | **Vitalis Heroes** | **Amara:** Golden-brown skin, dreadlocks in high ponytail with **golden bead cuff tips**, bright yellow athletic tank & shorts.<br>**Felix:** Short fade, **red athletic jersey with white chest block & stripes**, red shorts, red sneakers. | Missing Amara's gold bead cuffs or altering Felix's signature red jersey block. |
| **6** | **Luminosity & Aquaria** | **Athena & Ezra:** Royal purple academy uniforms with gold piping & purple neckties.<br>**Nerissa:** Seafoam tunic with **pink plumeria flower**.<br>**Ronan:** Teal shirt with ocean wave graphics. | Generic school uniforms or missing Nerissa's plumeria flower. |
| **7** | **Terrasol & Celestia** | **Vesta:** Green botanical tunic with two braided pigtails.<br>**Silas:** Green t-shirt under tan multi-pocket safari vest.<br>**Selene:** Silver-platinum hair & celestial stardust gown.<br>**Elias:** Midnight-blue kurta with gold sun/star embroidery. | Altering Vesta's botanical tunic or Silas's safari vest. |
| **8** | **Color Palette & Contrast** | Dominant **Cream `#FFF8F0`** background warmth with **Golden-Orange `#FF6F00`** accents. High mobile contrast (passes 3-second scroll test). | Muddy dark backgrounds, neon green/purple clash, low-contrast text. |
| **9** | **Racial Diversity of Non-Hero Children** | Any non-hero children, students, or family members depicted alongside the heroes **must be racially diverse** across all scenes (Black, Hispanic/Latino, East/South Asian, Indigenous, Mixed-race, Caucasian) with authentic skin tones, natural curly/coily/wavy/straight hair textures, and genuine joyful expressions. | Homogeneous single-ethnicity crowds, stereotypical caricatures, or whitewashed classroom groups. |
